# Time Value of Money and the Law of One Price

## Formulas
- Future Value: \( FV = PV(1+r)^n \)
- Present Value: \( PV = \frac{FV}{(1+r)^n} \)
- NPV of cash flows: \( NPV = \sum_{t=0}^{n}\frac{CF_t}{(1+r)^t} \)

## Questions
1. If ₹10,000 is invested at 8% for 3 years, what is the future value?
2. What is the present value of ₹15,000 received after 2 years at a discount rate of 10%?
3. Why does the Law of One Price rule out arbitrage in identical assets?

## Explanations
- The time value of money means money today is worth more than the same amount in the future because it can earn returns.
- Discounting converts future cash flows into present values so projects can be compared on a common basis.
- The Law of One Price states that identical cash flows must have the same price in efficient markets; otherwise arbitrage opportunities would exist.
