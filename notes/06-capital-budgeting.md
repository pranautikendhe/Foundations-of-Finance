# Capital Budgeting

## Formulas
- Net Present Value: \( NPV=\sum_{t=0}^{n}\frac{CF_t}{(1+r)^t} \)
- Internal Rate of Return: \( IRR \) is discount rate where \(NPV=0\)
- Payback Period: time needed to recover initial investment

## Questions
1. Why is NPV preferred over payback period?
2. A project has initial outlay ₹50,000 and discounted inflows totaling ₹62,000. Should it be accepted?
3. What causes multiple IRRs in some projects?

## Explanations
- Capital budgeting evaluates long-term investment decisions.
- NPV directly measures value addition and aligns with shareholder wealth maximization.
- IRR is intuitive but can mislead with non-conventional cash flows or mutually exclusive projects.
