# Capital Structure

## Formulas
- Weighted Average Cost of Capital: \( WACC=\frac{E}{V}R_e + \frac{D}{V}R_d(1-T_c) \)
- Debt-to-equity ratio: \( D/E=\frac{\text{Total Debt}}{\text{Total Equity}} \)

## Questions
1. How does the tax shield make debt financing attractive?
2. Why does excessive leverage increase financial distress risk?
3. How does WACC guide financing and investment decisions?

## Explanations
- Capital structure is the mix of debt and equity used to finance assets.
- Moderate debt can reduce overall cost of capital due to tax benefits.
- Beyond an optimal point, added debt raises required returns and bankruptcy risk.
