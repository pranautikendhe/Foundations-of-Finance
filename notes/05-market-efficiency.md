# Market Efficiency

## Formulas
- Excess return: \( \alpha_i = R_i - [R_f + \beta_i(R_m-R_f)] \)

## Questions
1. Distinguish weak, semi-strong, and strong forms of market efficiency.
2. Why are persistent abnormal returns difficult in efficient markets?
3. Can insider information produce abnormal returns under strong-form efficiency?

## Explanations
- Market efficiency means prices rapidly incorporate available information.
- In efficient markets, expected abnormal profits are competed away.
- Evidence generally supports weak and semi-strong forms more than strong form.
