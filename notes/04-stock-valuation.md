# Stock Valuation

## Formulas
- Dividend Discount Model (constant growth): \( P_0=\frac{D_1}{r-g} \)
- Price-to-earnings relationship: \( P_0 = (P/E) \times EPS \)

## Questions
1. A firm pays next dividend of ₹4, grows at 5%, and has required return 12%. What is intrinsic value?
2. When is the constant-growth DDM not appropriate?
3. How can relative valuation differ from intrinsic valuation?

## Explanations
- Stock valuation estimates fair value from expected future cash flows.
- DDM works best for mature dividend-paying firms with stable growth.
- Multiples (like P/E) are useful benchmarks but depend on comparable firm quality.
