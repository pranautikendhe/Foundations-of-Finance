# Payout Policy

## Formulas
- Dividend payout ratio: \( \frac{\text{Dividends}}{\text{Net Income}} \)
- Retention ratio: \( 1 - \text{Payout Ratio} \)
- Sustainable growth rate: \( g = ROE \times \text{Retention Ratio} \)

## Questions
1. Compare dividends and share repurchases as payout mechanisms.
2. How does payout policy affect investor signaling?
3. Why might high-growth firms retain a larger share of earnings?

## Explanations
- Payout policy determines how earnings are split between distributions and reinvestment.
- Dividends offer predictable cash flow; repurchases offer flexibility.
- Firms balance growth opportunities, shareholder preferences, taxes, and signaling effects.
