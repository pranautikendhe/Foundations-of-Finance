# Bond Pricing

## Formulas
- Bond price: \( P = \sum_{t=1}^{n}\frac{C}{(1+y)^t} + \frac{F}{(1+y)^n} \)
- Current yield: \( \text{Current Yield} = \frac{\text{Annual Coupon}}{\text{Bond Price}} \)

## Questions
1. How does bond price change when yield to maturity rises?
2. Calculate the price of a bond with face value ₹1,000, 8% annual coupon, 5 years to maturity, and 10% YTM.
3. Why do long-duration bonds react more strongly to interest-rate changes?

## Explanations
- A bond’s value is the present value of coupon payments plus face value repayment.
- Bond prices and yields move inversely.
- Longer maturity and lower coupon increase interest-rate sensitivity.
