# Diversification and CAPM

## Formulas
- Portfolio return: \( E(R_p)=\sum w_i E(R_i) \)
- Two-asset variance: \( \sigma_p^2=w_1^2\sigma_1^2+w_2^2\sigma_2^2+2w_1w_2\sigma_1\sigma_2\rho_{12} \)
- CAPM: \( E(R_i)=R_f+\beta_i(E(R_m)-R_f) \)

## Questions
1. How does low correlation between assets improve diversification?
2. What does beta measure in CAPM?
3. If \(R_f=6\%\), market risk premium is 5%, and \(\beta=1.2\), what is expected return?

## Explanations
- Diversification reduces unsystematic risk by combining assets that do not move perfectly together.
- CAPM links expected return to systematic risk (beta), not total risk.
- Higher beta implies higher required return for bearing greater market risk.
